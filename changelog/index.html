Changelog

Islang checks for updates every few hours and installs them for you.

0.16 Latest

November 26, 2026

Picture themes

New

  • Six new island themes with their own pictures: Candy (pink, with hearts and sparkles), Supercar, Space (with twinkling stars), Ocean, Neon city and Forest. The picture sits behind the top of the open island and fades into black. Settings, General.
  • A calmer tab row: icons only, with a soft circle on the current page.

0.15

November 26, 2026

Live activities

New

  • While the island is closed, running things sit beside it in small circles: the focus timer with its ring and time left, a coding agent at work or waiting for you, a meeting starting soon with a Join button, and keep-awake.
  • Click a circle to open its page. Music keeps its place in the island.
  • Turn them off in Settings, Island.

0.14

November 26, 2026

Instant open

New

  • The island opens the moment your pointer touches it, with no wait.
  • The Agents page finds Claude Code sessions that are already running, including ones started before Islang was connected and ones in the Claude app or an editor.

0.13

November 26, 2026

Permissions, privacy and livelier pets

New

  • A Permissions page in Settings shows what Islang uses, what is required and what is optional, with Allow buttons.
  • Setup continues only once Accessibility and Notifications are on. If one is turned off later, Islang opens the Permissions page.
  • Islang says it plainly: your data stays on your Mac, with no tracking, analytics or ads.
  • Drawn previews for the island themes.
  • Pets look at you on hover, and hop, dance or do their own trick when you click them.

Fixed

  • Copy, paste, cut, select all and undo work in the island's notes, tasks, search and replies.
  • The island stays open while you type in it.

0.12

November 26, 2026

Everything through the island

New

  • While Islang runs, macOS notification banners stay out of sight and every message shows on the island. Open and Reply still go to the exact chat. Turn it off in Settings, Notifications.
  • A blue Update button with the version number appears next to the language button when a new version is out. One click installs it and relaunches.

0.11

November 26, 2026

Smoother first run

New

  • On first launch the welcome comes first; the island appears after it, not on top of it.
  • If Islang can't read notifications yet, Home says so and has an Allow button.
  • New versions are announced on the island, in macOS notifications and in the menu bar, with a later reminder if you haven't installed them.

Fixed

  • Reply and Open sit side by side on message banners.
  • The focus countdown stays inside the island; the menu bar shows a small timer sign.

0.10

November 26, 2026

Make it yours

New

  • A new first run: your name, a generated pixel avatar, your pets, the permissions you want and a short tour of the main moves.
  • Island themes: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter or by season, plus the plain default. Settings, General.
  • A soft light runs along the sides and bottom of the island when a message arrives.
  • Messages that arrive while the island is open sit inside it instead of covering it.
  • Mirror: check yourself on camera before a call, from the Capture tab.
  • Notes: a scratchpad in the Clipboard tab.
  • Click the pet next to Islang to change it, or show the Islang logo instead.
  • Connect Claude Code right from the Agents page, and disconnect it in Settings.
  • New icons for Agents, Clipboard and Mac, and new buttons, switches and segmented controls throughout.

Fixed

  • ⌥⌘V and ⌥⌘K switched work modes instead of opening Clipboard and the command palette.
  • Approving Claude Code requests from the island stopped working after the rename.

0.9

November 26, 2026

Account and updates

New

  • An Account page in Settings with your name, email, friends and license in one place.
  • On Macs without a notch the island sits flush with the top of the screen. Pick a floating style or another display in Settings.
  • Updates install themselves while you are away, or with one click from the island.
  • Shuffle, repeat and love buttons in the music player.
  • Keep your Mac awake from the menu bar or the command palette.
  • Islang now asks for your license when it starts.

Fixed

  • Paste and other edit shortcuts work in the license key field.
  • The disk image opens with Islang.app, no longer the old name.
