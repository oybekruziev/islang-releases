Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Islang is built to keep your data on your Mac. There is no tracking, no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your Mac

  • Notifications shown on the island, clipboard history, tasks, reminders, focus stats and pet progress are stored only on your Mac.
  • Calendar events are read on your Mac to show them. They are never uploaded.
  • Sound levels for the music bars are measured on your Mac and are never recorded or sent.

What goes over the internet

  • Weather: your location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast.
  • Speed test: only when you start it, test data is exchanged with Cloudflare.
  • Updates: Islang asks GitHub whether a new version is available and downloads it from there.
  • License: your email, license key and your Mac's name are sent to Polar to sign you in and to check the license from time to time.
  • Friends: if you use sharing, your email, first name, the emails of friends you add and the items you share are stored on Islang's server, hosted by Cloudflare. Shared items are deleted after 7 days, or earlier when you delete them.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Polar (polar.sh), the merchant of record. We never see your card details.

Deleting your data

Sign out in Settings, Account to remove your license from the Mac. To delete your friends data from the server, write to r.oybek2001@gmail.com from the address you signed in with.

Who owns Islang

Islang, its code, design, pixel pets, name and website belong to Oybek Ruziev alone. Buying a license gives you the right to use the app; it does not transfer any ownership.

Changes to this policy

I may change this policy, the app and its features at any time and at my own discretion. The date at the top shows the latest version. Using Islang after a change means you accept it.

Contact
