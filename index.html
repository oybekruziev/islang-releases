Questions

Does it work on a Mac without a notch?

Yes. On an iMac, a Mac mini display or an older MacBook, the island sits flush with the top edge of the screen. In Settings you can choose a floating style or a different display.

How do I activate it?

Open Islang and enter the email you paid with and your license key. The key is in your Polar receipt email and in the customer portal.

Can I use it on two Macs?

One license is active on one Mac at a time. Sign out in Settings, Account to move it to another Mac.

Is there a free trial?

No trial, but there's a 14-day refund. If Islang isn't for you, write to us and you get your money back.

What does Islang collect?

Only your email and license to sign you in, and what you choose to share with friends. Music, calendar and clipboard stay on your Mac. Read the privacy policy.

Which macOS do I need?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
