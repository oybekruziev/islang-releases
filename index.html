Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes. On other Macs and external displays the island sits at the top centre of the screen and works the same way.

How do I sign in?

After purchase you get a license key. Open Islang → Settings → License, enter the e-mail you bought with and the key, then click Sign in. Can't find the key? Click "Where is my key?" — it opens your Polar customer portal.

I got a new Mac. Can I move my license?

Yes. A key works on one Mac at a time. Click Sign out in Islang on the old Mac (or free it in the Polar customer portal), then sign in on the new one.

Which permissions does it ask for?

Accessibility (volume, brightness and notification banners on the island), Notifications, Calendar and Location (only for weather, rounded to about 1 km). All optional — features that need one simply stay off.

How do updates work?

Islang checks a few times a day. When an update is ready it appears on the island — one click installs it, and every update is signature-checked first.

Privacy

Islang is built to keep your data on your Mac. No tracking, no analytics, no ads.

What stays on your Mac

  • Notifications shown on the island, clipboard history, tasks, reminders, focus stats and pet progress are stored only on your Mac.
  • Calendar events are read on your Mac to show them; they are never uploaded.
  • Sound levels for the music bars are measured on your Mac and never recorded or sent.

What goes over the internet

  • Weather: your location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to Open-Meteo for the forecast.
  • Speed test: only when you press it, test data is exchanged with Cloudflare.
  • Updates: Islang asks GitHub whether a new version exists.
  • License: your e-mail, license key and your Mac's name are sent to Polar to sign in and occasionally verify the license.
  • Friends: if you use sharing, your e-mail, first name, the e-mails of friends you add and what you share are stored on Islang's server (Cloudflare). Shared items are deleted automatically after 7 days, or earlier when you delete them.
