Questions

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes. On other Macs and on external displays the island sits at the top centre of the screen and works the same way.

Which permissions does it ask for, and why?

Accessibility (to show volume, brightness and notification banners on the island and to open the right chat), Notifications (for your own focus and reminder alerts), Calendar (to show your events), and Location (only for the weather — rounded to about 1 km). Every permission is optional; features that need one simply stay off.

How do I activate my license?

After purchase you get a key by email (and in your Polar customer portal). Open Islang → Settings → License, paste the key and click Activate.

I got a new Mac. Can I move my license?

Yes. A key works on 3 Macs at a time. You can free a Mac from the Polar customer portal, or click Deactivate in Islang's settings on the old Mac.

How do updates work?

Islang checks for a new version a few times a day. When one is ready it appears on the island — one click installs it. Every update is signed and verified before it replaces the app.

Something isn't working.

Open Settings → Report a problem. It opens an email with your Mac and app version filled in, so we can help fast.

Privacy

Islang is built to keep your data on your Mac. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your Mac

  • Notifications that Islang shows on the island, your clipboard history, tasks, reminders, focus stats and pet progress are stored only on your Mac.
  • Calendar events are read on your Mac to show them; they are never uploaded.
  • Sound levels for the music bars are measured on your Mac and are never recorded or sent.

What goes over the internet

  • Weather: your location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast.
  • Speed test: only when you press it, test data is downloaded from and uploaded to Cloudflare.
  • Updates: Islang asks GitHub whether a new version exists.
  • License: your license key and your Mac's name are sent to Polar to activate and occasionally verify the license.
